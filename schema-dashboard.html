BOM Workspace Construction planning

From tender to export

Upload and match a tender, analyze the draft BOM, then review and export the approved version.

Build plan

Start with the existing importer and catalogue. Test the full workflow on the supplied tender archive.

Available today · PostgreSQL, original-file storage, format parsers, draft BOMs, material products, and dated supplier prices.

  1. 01

    Harden upload and parsing

    Add a project upload screen and a background import job. Show sources and warnings. Fix the council’s findings on PDF columns, alternative links, source notes, and duplicate projections.

    Acceptance checkReimport the supplied ZIP without duplicate drafts or rates. Compare the 274 AN24-0305 prices and verify that alternatives survive promotion.

  2. 02

    Extend the catalogue for activities

    Keep material products and activity entries distinct. Add dated activity rates per work unit, with a supplier for subcontracted services and an internal rate source for in-house work.

    Acceptance check“Supply and lay pipe” retains one tender line linked to a pipe product and a separate laying activity.

  3. 03

    Match known entries first

    Use supplier SKUs and normalized specifications, units, and activity types. Store each match decision with the selected catalogue entry and rate. Queue unresolved items for research, including rate-only proposals for known entries.

    Acceptance checkA known pipe resolves to its applicable rate. An incompatible size, ambiguous result, or missing rate stays visible for review.

  4. 04

    Research and stage proposals

    Use GPT-6 Luna through the connected ChatGPT plan to search for supplier, description, category, currency, unit, price, dates, and source URLs. Store proposals and evidence with the unresolved item.

    Acceptance checkThe connected account can run Luna with web search. A missing price stays empty. A proposal remains pending until an engineer edits and approves it or selects an existing entry.

  5. 05

    Analyze scope, pricing, and coverage

    Run checks across matched and proposed entries. Flag missing or outdated rates, unit mismatches, unresolved alternatives, possible duplicate scope, and source clauses that affect cost or delivery. Group by project area, BOM section, material or activity category, and supplier where known.

    Acceptance checkEach flag points to the affected line and evidence. Group totals use compatible units and currencies, and show which lines still need prices.

  6. 06

    Review and approve the BOM

    Show matched, suggested, and unresolved rows with their risk flags and tender evidence. Save approval, catalogue creation or reuse, and the item link in one transaction. Record the selected rate and approval in the reviewed BOM version.

    Acceptance checkApproving twice creates one entry. A rejected proposal stays unresolved. Review catches missing required fields and conflicting options before final export.

  7. 07

    Export and test round trips

    Build Word and PDF templates and extend the readers to re-import them. Add GAEB XML 3.1/3.2 and GAEB 1990 profiles by phase, then add separately validated priced XML and ZIP packaging. Record the BOM version and profile used for each export.

    Acceptance checkExport and re-import a reviewed BOM per supported profile. Compare hierarchy, item codes, quantities, units, and permitted prices. Show a report of fields the target format cannot represent.
